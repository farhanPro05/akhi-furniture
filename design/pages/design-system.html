Akhi Furniture, on the web

The Akhi Furniture design system (colours, type, spacing, radii, logo) applied to the showroom website, plus the web additions: fluid type, a Bengali type pair, layout, components and their states. Tokens live in assets/tokens.css.

Space Grotesk (display) and Inter (text) from the design system. For Bengali, Anek Bangla pairs with Space Grotesk and Hind Siliguri with Inter. Bengali is set about 12% larger with taller lines, and never uppercase or letter-spaced. Sizes are fluid from phone to desktop.

Sections: 56 px apart on phones, 80 px (space-20) from 768 px.

Gutters: 16 phone · 24 from 600 · 32 from 1024 · 48 from 1440. Container caps at 1440 px, so 1920+ screens get calm side margins.

Grid gap: design cards 24 px (12 px between columns on phones).

Radii: 4 px chips, inputs, code chip · 8 px buttons, cards, image wells · pill only for filter chips · 16 px top corners of the phone bottom sheet.

Depth: 1 px line borders. shadow-card only on card hover, dropdown and sheets.

Breakpoints: 600 · 768 · 1024 · 1200 (full nav) · 1440.

Motion: 200 to 300 ms ease-out; image hover zoom 1.03 over 600 ms (desktop only); all off with reduced motion.

Icons

One outline set, 1.5 px stroke, rounded joins (Lucide in the build). 20 px in UI, 24 px in navigation. The WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons here are stand-ins: the build uses each brand's official glyph, always with a text label.

Logo

Header lockup (proposed): the AF mark at 34 to 42 px next to “Akhi Furniture” set in Space Grotesk 600. The stacked full logo becomes unreadable at header height, and the design system asks for the mark in tight spaces.
