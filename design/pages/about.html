Our story

Akhi Furniture began as a small workshop making beds and dining sets for families in Dhaka. TODO-VERIFY founding story & year

Today we design and build furniture for homes and offices, from a single bed to a full boardroom. Most of what we make is made to order: sized for the customer's room, in the wood and finish they choose.

We still work the way we started. Our own craftsmen cut, join, carve and finish every piece, and we show our real work, nothing else.
